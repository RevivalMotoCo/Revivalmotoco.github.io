Written Warranty

Every RMC retail motorcycle is supplied with a written limited warranty. Depending on the individual motorcycle, cover will be provided for 30, 60 or 90 days, subject to a maximum of 1,500 miles, whichever occurs first. The exact cover supplied will be stated in the motorcycle’s advert and sales documents.

Routine servicing, consumables and failures caused by normal wear and tear, misuse, accident damage or unauthorised modification are excluded. This warranty is additional to, and does not affect, your statutory rights.
