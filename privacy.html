Privacy notice

Revival Moto Co Ltd is responsible for the personal information we use. Contact us at info@revivalmotoco.co.uk.

What we collect and why

When you contact us or make a booking or purchase, we may receive your name, contact details, postcodes or addresses, motorcycle details, photographs and information about your enquiry, booking or order. We use it to reply, prepare quotes, arrange sales and services, and fulfil orders.

We use information to take steps you request before a contract and to fulfil our contracts. We rely on legitimate interests to handle general enquiries and maintain records needed to deal with disputes. We keep accounting records as required by law.

Who handles the information

Our website forms use FormSubmit to deliver messages and uploaded photos to our business email. FormSubmit says it retains submissions for 30 days and does not retain uploaded files in its submission archive. We use our email service to manage correspondence and share relevant order details with delivery providers where needed to fulfil orders.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and booking information for as long as needed to respond, complete the work or order and handle any follow-up or dispute. We keep transaction records for the period required by tax and accounting law.

Your rights

You can ask for access to your data or ask us to correct it. Depending on the circumstances, you can also ask us to delete, restrict or transfer it, or object to our use of it. Email us using the address above. You can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office.
