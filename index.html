Who We Are

A Bloke
With a
Workshop

My name's Conrad. I've had a lifelong obsession with motorcycles — from BSAs and Moto Guzzis to Aprilias and KTMs. Revival Moto Co is what happens when that obsession finally gets a proper workshop of its own.

Revival Moto Co is straightforward: I find motorcycles that have been neglected, ignored, or left to quietly decompose, and I make them right. All kinds — anything with a bit of character and a story worth continuing.

No fakery. No shortcuts. Every bike gets properly looked at before it goes anywhere near a for-sale ad. If I find something worth knowing about, you'll know about it.

Based in Shropshire, working out of a proper workshop, and doing the job the way we'd want it done if we were buying the bike ourselves.

How It Works

How Revival
Works

01

Find

We source interesting motorcycles — barn finds, private sellers, long-term storage. No auction fodder. No Cat N hiding behind a fresh tin of Hammerite.

02

Inspect

Every component gets looked at. We document what we find — the good, the bad, and the things that make you say words you wouldn't use in front of your nan.

03

Recommission

We fix what needs fixing. We don't try to make a bike into something it isn't — just make it right for what it is.

04

Ride & Test

Every bike gets ridden before it goes anywhere near a for-sale ad. If something reveals itself on the road, we deal with it.

05

Sell Honestly

You get the full picture — what we found, what we did, what we left alone and why. No surprises after you've handed over your money.

The RMC Difference

Buying An Old Bike
Shouldn't Be A Leap Of Faith

We don't hide the process. Every retail bike is inspected, recommissioned as needed, road tested and documented. You'll be able to see what we found, what we changed, what we left alone and why.

No cosmetic theatre covering mechanical neglect. No pretending a 25-year-old motorcycle is brand new. Just a properly sorted bike, an honest description and the evidence to back it up.

Sell Your Bike

Got One
We Should
Know About?

We're always looking for bikes to buy. Barn finds, long-term projects, machines that have been sitting around waiting for someone to care — that's exactly what we're after.

We're ideally looking for Japanese, Italian or British bikes, but we'll consider anything with a bit of character. If you think it's interesting, tell us about it.

We're based in Aston Munslow, Shropshire. Within 50 miles is ideal, but we'll travel for the right bike.

We'll need a V5 in the seller's name and the bike needs to be complete — not crashed or written off.
