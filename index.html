Our Story

A Lifelong
Obsession.
A Proper Place For It.

Revival Moto Co grew out of Conrad's lifelong obsession with motorcycles — from BSAs and Moto Guzzis to Aprilias and KTMs — and the idea that interesting motorcycles deserve proper attention.

RMC is a small independent business, not a faceless motorcycle dealership. The aim is simple: understand each bike, do the work it needs and offer the sort of buying experience a motorcycle enthusiast would want.

No fakery. No shortcuts. Just used motorcycles, good work and a belief that their next chapter matters as much as the last one.

How It Works

How Revival Works

01

Find

RMC sources interesting motorcycles through private sellers, word of mouth and specialist auctions. No salvage stock. No Cat N hiding behind a fresh tin of Hammerite.

02

Inspect

Each bike gets a thorough inspection. What turns up gets documented — the good, the bad, and the things that make you say words you wouldn't use in front of your nan.

03

Recommission

Each motorcycle gets what it needs. The point isn't to make it into something it isn't — it's to make it right for what it is.

04

Ride & Test

Every bike gets ridden before it goes anywhere near a for-sale advert. If something reveals itself on the road, it gets dealt with.

05

Sell Honestly

The buyer gets the full picture — what was found, what was done, and what was deliberately left alone and why.

The RMC Difference

Buying A Used Motorcycle
Shouldn't Be A Leap Of Faith

Used motorcycles have history. That's part of the appeal. The problem is when you don't know what that history has left behind.

RMC isn't trying to turn a 25-year-old motorcycle into a new one. The aim is to offer interesting bikes whose condition is understood — and be straight about exactly what you're buying.

No cosmetic theatre covering mechanical neglect. No pretending a motorcycle is something it isn't.
