REVIVAL MOTO CO — WEBSITE UPDATE NOTES

FORM ACTIVATION
The Sell Your Bike and Contact forms use FormSubmit because GitHub Pages is a static host and cannot send email or receive file uploads by itself.

After the updated site is live, make ONE test submission from the live website. FormSubmit will send an activation email to info@revivalmotoco.co.uk. Click its confirmation link. After activation, subsequent submissions are forwarded to that inbox.

Photo uploads:
- Sell Your Bike accepts up to 10 JPG/PNG/WebP photos.
- The browser blocks more than 10 files.
- Total attachment size is limited to 10MB by FormSubmit.

ADDING A MOTORCYCLE
1. Upload its photos to the repository.
2. Add one complete record to motorcycles.js.
3. The homepage uses that same record for Current Stock.
4. Its full page is motorcycle.html?id=THE-ID.
5. Changing status to sold removes it from Current Stock and places it in the hidden-until-used Sold Archive section.
6. A customer review only displays when review is populated AND reviewPermission is true.

The single motorcycles.js record is the canonical website source for price, mileage, model and derivative. Use the same record when writing Auto Trader, eBay and social adverts.
